## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Upload logo and product photos
`POST /api/brands/:id/assets` SHALL accept multipart form data with a `kind` field (`logo` or `product_photo`) and one or more `files` parts, and answer 201 with `{ assets }` listing the created assets. It SHALL answer 404 for an unknown brand and 400 for a missing or unknown `kind`, for no files, and for more than one file when `kind` is `logo`.

#### Scenario: Upload several photos
- **WHEN** three valid images are uploaded with `kind` `product_photo`
- **THEN** the response is 201 with three assets of kind `product_photo`

#### Scenario: Upload a logo
- **WHEN** one valid image is uploaded with `kind` `logo`
- **THEN** the response is 201 with one asset of kind `logo`

#### Scenario: Several files for a logo
- **WHEN** two files are uploaded with `kind` `logo`
- **THEN** the response is 400 and nothing is stored

#### Scenario: Unknown brand or kind
- **WHEN** the brand does not exist, or `kind` is `source_pdf`
- **THEN** the response is 404 for the brand and 400 for the kind

### Requirement: Image validation by content
Every uploaded image SHALL be detected from its content as PNG, JPEG, or WebP and SHALL be at most 10 MB; anything else SHALL yield 400. The stored `mime` and file extension SHALL come from the detected type, never from the client-supplied name or MIME type.

#### Scenario: Disguised file
- **WHEN** a file named `photo.png` with `Content-Type: image/png` but HTML content is uploaded
- **THEN** the response is 400 and nothing is stored

#### Scenario: Extension follows content
- **WHEN** a JPEG is uploaded under the file name `picture.png`
- **THEN** the stored asset has `mime` `image/jpeg` and a path ending in `.jpg`

#### Scenario: Image over the limit
- **WHEN** an image larger than 10 MB is uploaded
- **THEN** the response is 413 and nothing is stored

### Requirement: Request size limit
The endpoint SHALL answer 413 when `Content-Length` exceeds 60 MB, before reading the body.

#### Scenario: Oversized request
- **WHEN** the declared `Content-Length` is 61 MB
- **THEN** the response is 413

### Requirement: Photo limit and all-or-nothing uploads
A brand SHALL have at most 20 product photos. When an upload contains any invalid file, or would exceed the photo limit, the whole request SHALL be rejected (400) and no file or row SHALL be created.

#### Scenario: One bad file among good ones
- **WHEN** three files are uploaded and the second is not an image
- **THEN** the response is 400 and the brand still has no new asset or file

#### Scenario: Exceeding the photo limit
- **WHEN** a brand has 19 photos and two more are uploaded
- **THEN** the response is 400 and the brand still has 19 photos

#### Scenario: Exactly at the limit
- **WHEN** a brand has 19 photos and one more is uploaded
- **THEN** the response is 201 and the brand has 20 photos

### Requirement: Generated storage paths
Files SHALL be stored under `mediaDir` at `brands/<brandId>/logo-<assetId>.<ext>` for logos and `brands/<brandId>/products/<assetId>.<ext>` for photos, where `<assetId>` is generated by the server. The client-supplied file name SHALL NOT be used in any path. Each asset SHALL record its `size_bytes`.

#### Scenario: Path shape
- **WHEN** a photo named `../../evil.png` is uploaded
- **THEN** the stored path matches `brands/<brandId>/products/ast_<id>.png` and `size_bytes` equals the uploaded size

### Requirement: Single logo
A brand SHALL have at most one logo. Uploading a new logo SHALL replace the existing logo: the old row and file are removed and the new logo is the only logo.

#### Scenario: Replace the logo
- **WHEN** a second logo is uploaded
- **THEN** the brand has exactly one logo asset, the new one, and the old file no longer exists on disk

### Requirement: Ordering
New photos SHALL get a `sort_order` one higher than the current maximum for that brand and kind, in upload order. Assets SHALL be listed ordered by `sort_order` and then creation time.

#### Scenario: Append order
- **WHEN** two photos are uploaded in one request and a third in a later request
- **THEN** their sort orders increase in that order and the detail lists them in that order

### Requirement: Update an asset
`PATCH /api/brands/:id/assets/:assetId` SHALL accept `{ label?, sortOrder? }` where `label` is a string of at most 60 characters or null and `sortOrder` is a non-negative integer, update only the given fields, and answer 200 with the asset. It SHALL answer 400 for invalid values, 404 when the asset does not exist or belongs to another brand, and SHALL NOT apply to `source_pdf` assets (404).

#### Scenario: Label a photo
- **WHEN** a photo is patched with label `botol depan`
- **THEN** the asset has that label and its other fields are unchanged

#### Scenario: Label too long
- **WHEN** the label is 61 characters long
- **THEN** the response is 400

#### Scenario: Asset of another brand
- **WHEN** an asset id from brand A is patched through brand B
- **THEN** the response is 404

### Requirement: Delete an asset
`DELETE /api/brands/:id/assets/:assetId` SHALL remove the row and the file of a logo or product photo and answer 204. It SHALL answer 404 when the asset does not exist, belongs to another brand, or is a `source_pdf`.

#### Scenario: Delete a photo
- **WHEN** a photo is deleted
- **THEN** the response is 204, the row is gone, and the file no longer exists

#### Scenario: Source PDF is protected
- **WHEN** the `source_pdf` asset of a PDF brand is deleted through this endpoint
- **THEN** the response is 404 and the asset remains

### Requirement: Asset representation
Assets SHALL be returned as `{ id, kind, url, mime, sizeBytes, label, sortOrder }`, where `url` is `/media/` followed by the stored path.

#### Scenario: URL is served
- **WHEN** an uploaded photo's `url` is requested
- **THEN** the media route returns the uploaded bytes with the detected content type
